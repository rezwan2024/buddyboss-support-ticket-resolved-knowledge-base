## Debugging steps
1. Checked the error in the screenshot. It comes from Apple, not from the app builder.
2. Ruled out: the Release profile. App Store distribution profiles carry no device list, so only the Test App profile fails.
3. Checked the Apple Developer Devices list with the platform filter set to show all devices, not just iOS.
4. Found non-iOS devices (Mac, Apple TV, Apple Watch or Vision Pro, or UUID style identifiers with hyphens) that Apple rejects in an iOS profile.

## Solution
In the Apple Developer account, open Devices, show all platforms and disable every Mac, Apple TV, Apple Watch and Apple Vision Pro entry (devices cannot be removed before the membership renews, but disabled ones are left out). Keep at least one enabled iPhone or iPad. Then in the app iOS settings click Generate New Profiles, save and start a fresh iOS build.
Root cause: a non-iOS device, often an Apple silicon Mac added by Xcode, was registered on the Apple Developer team, so Apple rejected the whole development profile request.
