## Debugging steps
1. Checked the build log of the failed iOS dev build.
2. Found the development provisioning profile in the Apple Developer account had expired.
3. Ruled out: app settings or changes on the site as the cause.

## Solution
Renew or regenerate the expired development provisioning profile in the Apple Developer account under Certificates, Identifiers & Profiles. If its signing certificate has also expired, renew the certificate first. Then request a new iOS dev build.
Root cause: expired iOS development provisioning profile used for the build.
