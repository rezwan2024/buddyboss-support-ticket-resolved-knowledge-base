## Debugging steps
1. Confirmed the left BuddyPanel and the right sidebar are both enabled.
2. Explained that the middle content area gets narrower by design when both are active.

## Solution
Disable one of the sidebars on the affected pages (for example profile pages), or add custom CSS to adjust the widths and margins of `.bb-buddypanel` and `.sidebar`. This is a layout adjustment, not a bug fix.
Root cause: by design, the left BuddyPanel and the right sidebar both take space from the center content column.
