## Debugging steps
1. Registered a new account as a normal visitor with a Gmail address: the form completed but no activation email arrived.
2. Asked the site owner to repeat the test with a Gmail address, since their own tests used other addresses.
3. Suggested temporarily disabling the newly added bot protection and registering again to see if it blocks the activation flow.
4. Suggested an SMTP plugin with email logging to see if the activation email was sent, failed, or never triggered.

## Solution
Send site mail through SMTP (for example FluentSMTP with Gmail, Brevo or the host's SMTP) instead of the default PHP mail function, then check the email log after a fresh registration.
If the log shows no activation email was triggered, the custom bot protection is stopping registration before the email step and must be adjusted or removed.
The customer fixed it on their own side and did not share the exact change, so treat these as the recommended checks, not a confirmed fix.
Root cause: not confirmed; most likely the custom bot protection changes or blocked default server mail.
