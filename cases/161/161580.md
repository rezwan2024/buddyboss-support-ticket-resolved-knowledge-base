## Debugging steps
1. Checked the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy page assignments under BuddyBoss > Settings > Appearance > Pages (Registration Pages). Ruled out: both pages were assigned and published.
2. Ruled out: both pages opened correctly in a web browser, and the Privacy Policy page loaded in the app, so the problem was only the Terms of Service page.
3. Installed the release build from the BuddyBoss App dashboard: the Terms of Service window opened with only the cookie consent banner and no page content.
4. Opened the Terms of Service page in the editor and found it was built with Thrive Architect. The editor shows a notice that content the app does not support falls back to the web version.

## Solution
Rebuild the Terms of Service page with the standard WordPress editor (no page builder), publish it and select it in the Terms of Service field under BuddyBoss > Settings > Appearance > Pages. On a private site, add the new page URL to Public Website Content under BuddyBoss > Settings > Advanced > Privacy. Then fully close and reopen the app.
Root cause: the app could not render the page builder content of the Terms of Service page, so the window stayed blank.
