## Debugging steps
1. Confirmed Gamification was enabled after the community already had a long history of activity.
2. Checked for a built-in backfill tool or official script. Gamification has none. It only tracks activity from the moment it is enabled.

## Solution
Award points and badges by hand in WP Admin > BuddyBoss > Gamification > Members: select the member and use the manual award option (see the "Manually Award or Deduct Points in BuddyBoss Gamification" doc). This is a workaround and fits a small number of key members.
Backfilling rewards for many members needs custom development that reads past activity and awards points through code.
Root cause: Gamification has no retroactive tracking and only counts activity after it is enabled.
