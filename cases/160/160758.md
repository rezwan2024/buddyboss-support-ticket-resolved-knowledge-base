## Debugging steps
1. Read the request: a profile Shop tab using the Documents layout, member balance on top, Add Product and Create Section buttons, and each product published as an Activity post that holds all likes, comments and shares.
2. Ruled out: a bug or a setting. BuddyBoss has no store feature in member profiles, so this is a new feature request.
3. Ruled out: third-party marketplace add-ons. The customer had tried them and said they did not feel like a natural fit.

## Solution
Thank the customer and explain that the support ticket form is for troubleshooting existing features. Ask them to send the feature request to the product team through the BuddyBoss contact page: under "Got a basic question?" click "Complete A Form" and paste the proposal there.
Root cause: Not a bug. The feature does not exist in BuddyBoss and was sent to the wrong queue.

No reply after the answer. The ticket closed for inactivity.
