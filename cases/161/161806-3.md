## Debugging steps
1. Opened the app signup screen and confirmed the trial product shows as "Free Lifetime".
2. Ruled out: the PMPro membership level settings and BuddyBoss settings as the source of the label.
3. Traced the text to the in-app product title in App Store Connect and Google Play Console.

## Solution
Change the in-app product title in both App Store Connect and Google Play Console to something like "7-Day Free Trial". Then go to BuddyBoss App > In-App Purchases > Products, sync each product, and force close and reopen the app to see the new title.
Root cause: the label comes from the product title set in the app stores, not from BuddyBoss.
