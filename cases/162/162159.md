## Debugging steps
1. Reproduced from a member account: Profile, Membership tab, Change Plan, pick a paid plan, Select Plan. The checkout failed with a Stripe checkout error.
2. Confirmed as a bug and sent to development. A patch was prepared.
3. Applied the patch to the BuddyBoss Membership plugin files on the live site with the customer's permission. The checkout then loaded fully with the total, the Stripe card fields and the Submit button, and no errors were logged.
4. Opened the checkout pages of the other paid plans directly. All showed the payment step correctly. Asked the customer for a real test purchase because Stripe was in live mode.

## Solution
Update BuddyBoss Membership to 1.14.3 or later. That release includes the fix for the Stripe checkout error. Then run a real checkout test on a paid plan.
Before the release, a manual patch to the Membership plugin files worked. Do not update the plugin over a manual patch until the fixed version is out, because the update overwrites the patched files.
Root cause: a bug in the BuddyBoss Membership Stripe checkout broke the payment step for paid plans. It was fixed in 1.14.3.

## Customer note
Said the issue is gone after the update and thanked the team.
