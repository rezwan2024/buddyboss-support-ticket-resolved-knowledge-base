## Debugging steps
1. Reproduced as the document owner: open the document 3-dot menu and click Move. The folder picker popup does not open.
2. Ruled out: browser console errors. None show when Move is clicked, and it happens in the latest Chrome.
3. Confirmed it is a known Platform bug and sent it to the developers.

## Solution
Update BuddyBoss Platform to 3.6.0 or later and BuddyBoss Addons to 1.2.1 or later. The fix is in these versions.
Root cause: a Platform bug stopped the move dialog from opening for document owners. Fixed in Platform 3.6.0 and Addons 1.2.1.
