## Debugging steps
1. Checked the customer's subscription: it includes the Theme, Platform Pro and the add-ons, so no new purchase was needed.
2. Ruled out: licence connection on the site, it was connected correctly.
3. Found that polls and reactions no longer ship inside Platform Pro; they moved into the separate BuddyBoss Add-ons plugin, which was not installed.

## Solution
Download the BuddyBoss Add-ons plugin zip from the downloads section of the BuddyBoss account, upload it in Plugins, Add New, Upload Plugin, then install and activate it. Clear the caching plugin, host cache and CDN cache, then reload and check polls and reactions. Installing it changes no existing activity, polls or settings.
Root cause: polls and reactions moved out of Platform Pro into the separate BuddyBoss Add-ons plugin, so they stopped working until that plugin was installed.

## Customer note
Thanked the team for the clarification, installed the Add-ons plugin and said polls and reactions were working.
