## Debugging steps
1. Signed up in the app and confirmed an activation email is required before login.
2. Ruled out: a BuddyBoss setting to skip account activation. There is none.
3. Ruled out: the usual code workaround that skips activation. It only applies to website signups, because app signups use their own registration process.

## Solution
No setting or workaround exists for app signups. Members who register in the app still need to activate their account from the email before they can log in.
Root cause: app registration runs separately from website registration and always requires email activation.
