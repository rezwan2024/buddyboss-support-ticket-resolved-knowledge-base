## Debugging steps
1. Confirmed the customer was logged into the account that holds the license.
2. Licenses page showed an active Theme license, but Downloads listed only plugins.
3. Checked the account on the BuddyBoss side: the license was fine, the Theme was missing from Downloads due to a glitch on the account.

## Solution
Workaround: sent the latest buddyboss-theme.zip to the customer directly so the update could go ahead. The account was then fixed on the BuddyBoss side so the Theme shows in Downloads again.
Root cause: Account glitch that hid the Theme from the Downloads list while the license stayed active.

## Customer note
Asked if the Theme missing from Downloads was fixed. No reply after the team said it was resolved.
