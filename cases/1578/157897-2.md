## Debugging steps
1. Reviewed the Apple rejection message about Sign in with Apple.
2. Checked the app login screen: with a Login Background image set, Sign in with Apple and other SSO buttons appear transparent.
3. Confirmed it as a known bug and reported it to the development team.

## Solution
Workaround: go to BuddyBoss App > Appearance > Branding, under Login Screen remove the Login Background image and save. No rebuild needed; check the login screen on a device, then ask Apple to review the app again. Add the background back once the bug is fixed.
Root cause: bug where a login screen background image makes the SSO buttons render transparent.
