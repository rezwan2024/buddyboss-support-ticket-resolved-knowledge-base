## Debugging steps
1. Opened the membership product page: the order summary loads, then the payment form area shows the critical error.
2. Checked the PHP error log: fatal error from BbmsOrderQuoteBuilder basket_fingerprint(), called by BbmsStripeGateway get_elements_options() and display_on_site_form() while rendering the Stripe Elements form.
3. Searched the plugin files: the class BbmsStripeTaxIntegration is referenced in BbmsOrderQuoteBuilder.php and BbmsStripeGateway.php but is not defined anywhere in the 1.14.1 or 1.14.2 package.
4. Ruled out: Stripe Tax settings and site configuration. The class is called on every checkout even with Stripe Tax disabled.
5. Confirmed as a packaging bug in the Membership release. A 500 error on the membership plan page for logged-in users was reported at the same time and may come from the same missing class.

## Solution
Update BuddyBoss Membership to 1.14.3 or later (Dashboard > Updates or Plugins > Installed Plugins). Then click Connect with Stripe again under Memberships > Settings > Payments and run a test checkout in Stripe test mode.
Root cause: Membership 1.14.1 and 1.14.2 shipped without the BbmsStripeTaxIntegration class file, so every Stripe checkout render and submit hit a fatal error.
