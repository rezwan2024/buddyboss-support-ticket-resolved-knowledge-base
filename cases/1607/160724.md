## Debugging steps
1. Checked the ticket content: only an automated alert that a site visitor asked to talk to a person, with a link to the chat tool's dashboard.
2. Ruled out: a BuddyBoss technical issue. The alert came from the site's own third-party chat tool, which sends handoff alerts to several email addresses, including the BuddyBoss support address.

## Solution
Tell the site owner the visitor conversation is in their own chat dashboard and only their team can reply there. To stop these alerts, remove the BuddyBoss support email from the chat tool's notification recipients.
Root cause: the support email was added as a notification recipient in the site's third-party chat tool.
