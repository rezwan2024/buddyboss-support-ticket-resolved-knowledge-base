## Debugging steps
1. Checked BuddyBoss > Settings > Notifications > Notification Types: the Zoom meeting or webinar scheduled in a group notification and the new post by a followed member notification were on with Email and Web ticked.
2. Found the "new activity post in a group a member is subscribed to" row with Email and Web greyed out, because group subscriptions were not enabled.
3. Ruled out: a setting that forces a notification on and locks members out. Admins can only switch a type on or off site-wide; members control the rest in their own Notification Preferences.
4. Tested enabling group subscriptions: members already in a group are not subscribed retroactively; new members are subscribed automatically.

## Solution
Enable group subscriptions in BuddyBoss > Settings > Groups (Subscription section), then tick Email and Web on the "new activity post in a group a member is subscribed to" notification. Existing members subscribe from the bell icon on the group page. For Zoom meetings scheduled through the Zoom integration in a group, use the separate Zoom group notification.
For a message every member must get with no opt out, use a newsletter or email campaign to the member list (workaround).
Root cause: notifications are member controlled by design; there is no mandatory or locked notification option and no bulk apply to existing members.
