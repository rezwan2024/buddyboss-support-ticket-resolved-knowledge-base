## Debugging steps
1. Customer could not create a poll from the web activity feed or from the app, even with different poll options.
2. Customer fixed it on their own before support replied and did not say how.

## Solution
Customer fixed it on their own, and the cause was not shared. If it happens again, check that the Polls feature is turned on in the BuddyBoss settings and that each answer option is 1 to 50 characters long. Too long an option gives a length error.
Poll Duration, multiple answers and member-suggested options are all set in the same poll window.
