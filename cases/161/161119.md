## Debugging steps
1. Read the request as a presale question about custom development, not a support issue with an existing product.
2. Found the form had "Done For You Service" selected, so it was routed to technical support and not to the Agency team.
3. Ruled out: a support answer on feasibility or cost. Custom app work, quotes and engagement terms are handled only by the Agency team.

## Solution
Sent the customer to the Agency contact form on the BuddyBoss website, with "Agency" selected under "Which product are you contacting us about?", and the same questions pasted into the project description. Also informed the Agency team lead about the request.
Told the customer the first Agency assessment call is free with no obligation. Push notifications, native device features and publishing under the client's own Apple and Google developer accounts are all part of Agency app work. Feasibility and cost of each approach are decided with the Agency team.
Root cause: Custom development requests sent with the wrong product selected on the contact form go to support instead of the Agency team.

## Customer note
No reply after being sent to the Agency form. The ticket closed for inactivity.
