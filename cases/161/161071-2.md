## Debugging steps
1. Customer reported course and lesson pages crash after updating Tutor LMS to the latest version.
2. Checked with the development team: known compatibility issue with Tutor LMS 4.x.
3. Tutor LMS 4.x changed the structure of its course, lesson and dashboard pages, so the BuddyBoss layouts for them need rebuilding.

## Solution
Workaround: keep the live site on the current Tutor LMS version below 4.x until the BuddyBoss compatibility update is released. The rebuilt layouts were completed and in code review and testing at the time.
Customers who reported it earlier are linked to the fix and get notified on release.
Root cause: Tutor LMS 4.x changed its course, lesson and dashboard page structure, breaking the BuddyBoss layouts for those pages.

Thanked the team and said it is good to know the fix is on its way.
