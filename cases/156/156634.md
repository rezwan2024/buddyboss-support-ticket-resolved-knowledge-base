## Debugging steps
1. Asked whether the app is the Classic app or the Next Gen app. It is Next Gen.
2. Checked the App Codex APIs the customer found (NavigationApi, DeeplinksApi, InappProductsApi, ProductsScreen, ProductSingleScreen). They were written for the Classic app only and do not carry over to Next Gen.
3. Ruled out: a supported route or API for Next Gen. Custom development and developer APIs are not open yet for the Next Gen app.

## Solution
Custom development is not available on the Next Gen app yet, so there is no supported way to open the native Products or Product Single screen from custom code. Follow the BuddyBoss App release notes, where custom development and its docs will be announced once Next Gen is stable (no ETA).
If custom development is needed now, the Classic app supports it through the App Codex APIs. Switching back to Classic needs a new build and a new App Store and Google Play submission, and work done on Classic will likely need to be redone after moving to Next Gen.
Root cause: the App Codex developer APIs only apply to the Classic app, and the Next Gen app has no public custom development APIs yet.

## Customer note
Thanked the team for the honest answer, asked where to follow Next Gen updates and said they might switch back to the Classic app.
