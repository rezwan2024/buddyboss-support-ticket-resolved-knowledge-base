## Debugging steps
1. Ruled out: Elementor support for the post type, the membership products were already enabled in Elementor settings.
2. Ruled out: a general Elementor problem, all other Elementor pages opened in the editor.
3. Enabled Elementor > Tools > Safe Mode and opened a membership product with Edit with Elementor: instead of loading, it redirected to the Posts list.
4. Ruled out: SEO plugin redirect rules, none matched the product.
5. Checked that the product opens in the normal WordPress editor (it did) and opened the product on the front end in a private window to see if it redirects or shows a 404, since that keeps the Elementor preview iframe spinning.
6. Checked the browser console Network tab for failed or pending requests while the editor was stuck.

## Solution
The customer found the cause on their own site during these checks and did not share what it was.
Use the steps above: if the product front end redirects or fails, or the editor redirects in Safe Mode, fix that redirect or the conflicting plugin and Elementor will load.
Root cause: not shared; the Safe Mode redirect points to a conflict or redirect on the membership product, not to Elementor itself.
