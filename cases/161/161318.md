## Debugging steps
1. Ruled out: missing plugin. Platform and Platform Pro were both installed and active.
2. Disconnected the license key, flushed Redis object cache and page, server and CDN caches, then reconnected the Launch key. No change for the customer.
3. Checked the BuddyBoss account: correctly on the Web Launch plan with active updates.
4. Site firewall (Cloudflare, set per domain) blocked support access; the customer allowed the support country for that domain.
5. In the BuddyBoss Add-ons section the site was connected to Launch. The locked items (Gamification, Member Blogging, Offload Media) were labeled "Upgrade Scale", meaning they belong to the Scale plan, not that the site thought it was on Start.
6. Found the polls feature switched off in the BuddyBoss settings.

## Solution
Turn on the polls option in the BuddyBoss settings; after that the poll option shows and members can post polls. Explain that "Upgrade Scale" labels on add-ons are normal on a Launch license, since those add-ons come with the higher Scale plan.
Root cause: the polls setting was disabled on the site, and the Scale-only add-on labels were read as the site showing the Start plan.

## Customer note
Said it sounds good, thanked the team and confirmed they can post polls now.
