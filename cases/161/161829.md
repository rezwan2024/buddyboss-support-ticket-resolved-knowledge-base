## Debugging steps
1. Read the ticket: it was a member newsletter about new app features and holiday reminders, with no question for BuddyBoss.
2. Ruled out: any product issue or support request in the message.

## Solution
Replied that the message looks like a community newsletter sent to BuddyBoss support by mistake, and invited the customer to open a new ticket for any platform or app question. The customer removed the BuddyBoss support email from their mailing list.
Root cause: the BuddyBoss support email address was subscribed to the customer's member newsletter list.

## Customer note
Said that was exactly what happened, thanked the team and will remove the support email from the list.
