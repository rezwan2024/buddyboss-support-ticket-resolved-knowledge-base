## Debugging steps
1. Customer reinstalled Platform, Platform Pro, Add-ons and Theme fresh from the account and activated the licence again; activation reported success, but the site still could not verify it.
2. Checked the licence on the BuddyBoss side: an activation exists for the site's domain and it is the correct key.
3. Ruled out: subscription coverage, it includes Platform Pro and Add-ons, with no expiry or grace period ending.
4. Ruled out: feature loss, polls, reactions and the other Pro features were still working.

## Solution
No licence change or reissued key was needed. The message after a successful activation was treated as a caching issue during licence verification; the site was used as normal, and the licence then showed as enabled with Platform Pro and Add-ons covered.
Root cause: cached licence verification status after reactivation, not a problem with the licence itself.

## Customer note
Said it is resolved, the licence shows as enabled with all products covered, and thanked the team.
