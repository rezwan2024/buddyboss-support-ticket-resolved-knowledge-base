## Debugging steps
1. Read the message: a promotional newsletter from an external training company about webinars and courses.
2. Ruled out: any question about BuddyBoss Platform, Theme, App or account.

## Solution
Replied that the email reached the support address by mistake and invited the sender to open a new ticket if they have a BuddyBoss question, then closed the ticket.
Root cause: a third-party mailing list sent its newsletter to the support address.

## Customer note
No reply after the agent's answer.
