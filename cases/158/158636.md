## Debugging steps
1. Read the Apple rejection: the iOS build declares the audio background mode but reviewers found no persistent audio feature.
2. Checked BuddyBoss App settings on the site: Background Media Playback was enabled under Permissions.
3. Confirmed this option lets media keep playing when the app is minimized and, on iOS, adds the audio background mode at build time.

## Solution
If background playback is not needed: in the BuddyBoss App settings under Permissions, untick "Allow your app to play media in the background", save, create a new iOS build and resubmit, telling Apple the audio background mode was removed. The setting applies to both iOS and Android on the next build.
If background playback is needed: keep the option enabled and resubmit with a screen recording from a physical device showing background audio playing after going to the Home Screen, added in the App Review Information notes.
Root cause: the Background Media Playback permission adds the audio background mode to the iOS build, and Apple requires proof of a persistent audio feature.

## Customer note
Thanked the team, resubmitted with a video showing background audio and asked to close the ticket as resolved.
