## Debugging steps
1. Reproduced: user A blocks user B, B still sees A's posts and comments in the feed and in groups and can comment and react.
2. A reply by B to A's content tags "@undefined" instead of the name. Profile access was blocked correctly.
3. Confirmed as a known Platform bug and sent to the developers.
4. After the fix release, asked to update Platform, Platform Pro and the Theme and clear all caches, then recheck.

## Solution
Update BuddyBoss Platform to 3.5.1 or later, also update Platform Pro and the Theme, then clear all caches.
By design after the fix: blocking hides content in the main newsfeed and the profile feed. When both users are members of the same group, the blocked user can still see and react to the blocking user's group posts. A blocked user who opens the blocking user's profile gets a 404 page.
Root cause: a Platform bug where the blocked user's content was not filtered out of feeds and groups.

## Customer note
Said it is much better after the update, accepted the group behavior explanation and asked to close the ticket.
