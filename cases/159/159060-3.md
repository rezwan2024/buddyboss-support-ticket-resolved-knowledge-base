## Debugging steps
1. Checked the account licenses and order history for the App and Web Plus subscriptions.
2. Confirmed the Web Plus subscription was a separate purchase for the website.

## Solution
The App renewal price stays the same as long as the App subscription stays active; no new license or plan change is needed. BuddyBoss Web Plus is a separate website product, not required for the BuddyBoss App, and renews at its regular price after the introductory first-year price.
Root cause: customer was unsure whether the renewal rate was protected and whether Web Plus was tied to the App.
