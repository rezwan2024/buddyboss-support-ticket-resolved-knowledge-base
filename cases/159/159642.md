## Debugging steps
1. Compared the Next Gen app with the Legacy app on the same downloaded lesson video.
2. Ruled out: course setup and site settings. The same lesson not downloaded shows the Vimeo player with play button and controls.
3. Reproduced it on the customer's app. Only downloaded courses show the still frame with no controls until the first tap.
4. Escalated to the App team, who confirmed it is the expected behavior of the new player.

## Solution
Not a bug. In a recent update the Next Gen app switched downloaded videos on iOS to Apple's native video player, which by design hides its controls until the video is tapped once. One tap shows the play button and full control bar. The team keeps Apple's standard behavior to avoid App Store review problems. A visible play icon before the first tap was logged as feedback for the product team.
Root cause: the Next Gen app plays downloaded videos on iOS with Apple's native player, which hides controls until the first tap. The Legacy app uses a different player.

## Customer note
Said the native player is what they wanted, suggested a play icon so it does not look like an image, and thanked the team.
