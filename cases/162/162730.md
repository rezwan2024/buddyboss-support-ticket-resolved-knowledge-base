## Debugging steps
1. Logged in to the admin dashboard and checked for the Apple credentials notice: it was not showing for the agent.
2. Ruled out: Apple Social Login disabled. It was still enabled alongside Google under the Social Login settings.
3. Asked the customer to clear the browser cache and the site cache, including the Object Cache option in the admin bar, then reload the dashboard.

## Solution
Clearing the browser cache, the site cache and the object cache removed the notice; Apple login kept working.
When the notice really appears (Apple limits the Apple Sign-In token to six months), go to BuddyBoss > Settings > Login & Registration > Registration, open the three-dot menu on the Apple card under Social Login, delete the current credentials, enter the Apple details again and verify. Existing members keep their accounts.
Root cause: the cache held an older copy of the dashboard, so the expired credentials notice kept showing after the state had already changed.

## Customer note
Said the notice disappeared after clearing all caches, everything works, and thanked the team for the support.
