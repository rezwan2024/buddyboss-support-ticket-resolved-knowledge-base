## Debugging steps
1. Customer reported that lesson pages with Classic blocks holding HTML and shortcodes load in the app through the web fallback.
2. In that fallback the page scroll breaks and the LearnDash lesson list shows twice.
3. Confirmed that the Classic block always renders through WebView in the app, which is where both problems come from.

## Solution
No fix in this ticket. The customer was asked to open a separate ticket for the scrolling and duplicate lesson list problem so it could be checked on its own. Avoiding Classic blocks with shortcodes on app lesson pages avoids the web fallback.
Root cause: not found in this ticket; the problems appear only when the Classic block content renders through the app's WebView fallback.
