## Debugging steps
1. Customer tried to activate the product license on the BuddyBoss License Activation screen and got the validation errors message.
2. Asked for the license key, site URL and admin access to check the license and the activation on the site.
3. Checked the license and its activation from the BuddyBoss side and on the site's license screen.

## Solution
Support checked the license with the customer's key and access and activated it on the site, then asked the customer to check again. The exact change was not recorded in the ticket. If it happens again, check in the BuddyBoss account under Subscriptions whether the key is already active on another domain, deactivate it there and paste the key again with no extra spaces.
Root cause: Not recorded in the ticket; the license could not be validated for the site until support fixed the activation.

No reply after the fix; ticket closed for inactivity.
