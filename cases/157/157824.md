## Debugging steps
1. Customer saw social login disappear from the login and register forms and a crown icon with a Start prompt in its settings.
2. Ruled out: missing plan access. The customer's plan still includes social login.
3. Confirmed social login was moved out of the Platform into the separate BuddyBoss Addon plugin, which was not installed on the site.

## Solution
Download the BuddyBoss Addon plugin from the Downloads section of the BuddyBoss account, upload and activate it under Plugins > Add New > Upload Plugin. Then in the BuddyBoss license settings disconnect the license and connect it again with the same key, and reload the social login settings page. Google and Apple sign in become selectable again with the existing configuration.
Root cause: Social login moved into the separate BuddyBoss Addon plugin, so it stays locked until that plugin is installed and the license is reconnected.
