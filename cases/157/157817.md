## Debugging steps
1. Read the request: the sender holds back the report details until the reporting channel and any reward policy are confirmed.
2. Ruled out: no product, version or steps were given, so there was nothing to reproduce or test on support.

## Solution
Send the reporter to the official BuddyBoss security reporting page and ask them to submit the full report through the form there. The security team reviews it and replies directly with next steps, including any recognition or incentives for valid findings. Support does not promise rewards in the ticket.
Root cause: Not a product fault; a security report was sent to the general support form instead of the dedicated security reporting channel.
