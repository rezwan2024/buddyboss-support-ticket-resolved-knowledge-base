## Debugging steps
1. Customer updated all BuddyBoss plugins and made a new app build; the group Invite + button was still missing.
2. Confirmed this is a different feature from the profile email invites (group invites vs. site email invites).
3. Checked the group settings: group invitations were restricted to organizers and moderators, so regular members do not see the invite button.
4. Customer is a group organizer and still did not see the button, so it was treated as a product issue under a separate ticket.

## Solution
No fix in this ticket. First check the group's invitation setting: when it is limited to organizers and moderators, regular members will not see the invite button. If an organizer still cannot see it in the Next Gen app, it is a known product issue handled by the dev team; follow up in a separate ticket.
Root cause: not confirmed; group invite permission explains it for members, the organizer case was an open Next Gen app issue.

## Customer note
Said a separate ticket for the group invite issue was already open and unresolved, and bumped it.
