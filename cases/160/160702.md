## Debugging steps
1. Loaded the site from outside: pages, menus and course listings worked, no warning page or redirect.
2. Asked for the full scanner email and the Platform and Theme versions to check the alert is genuine and not phishing.
3. Alert came from Jetpack Scan and flagged BuddyBoss Platform up to 3.1.0 with the status "No known fix".
4. The development team confirmed the flagged issue was fixed in Platform 3.5.0, with a related fix in 3.6.0.
5. The customer was already on Platform 3.6.0 and Jetpack still flagged the plugin.

## Solution
Update BuddyBoss Platform to 3.6.0 or later from Dashboard > Plugins. Nothing else is needed. Jetpack may keep flagging the plugin until its database records the fix, so tell the customer the later alert is a delayed listing.
Root cause: Real scanner report fixed in Platform 3.5.0 and 3.6.0, but the scanner database had not recorded the fixed version yet.

## Customer note
Thanked the team, confirmed Platform 3.6.0 is installed and asked if any more action was needed.
