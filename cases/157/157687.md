## Debugging steps
1. Customer saw a short filter list for members who had only accepted a connection request and the full Activity Feed filter list for members who had sent one that was accepted.
2. Checked Dashboard > BuddyBoss > Settings > Activity, Activity Feed Filters section.
3. Found the individual filter options switched off, so members were offered only the reduced set on the Community Feed.

## Solution
In Dashboard > BuddyBoss > Settings > Activity > Activity Feed Filters, enable the filters members should see (All Updates, Created by Me, I've Reacted To, From My Connections, I'm Mentioned In, I'm Following, Unanswered) and drag the rows to set their order. Hard refresh and recheck with both members.
Root cause: most Activity Feed filter options were disabled in settings, not a rule tied to who sent the connection request.
