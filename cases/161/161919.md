## Debugging steps
1. Checked the known iOS issue where the Open button on document previews did nothing (started in app version 3.2.0, fixed in 3.2.3). Ruled out: it was a different PDF issue, not this one.
2. Tested PDF files in the customer's app and in a BuddyBoss development app side by side. Both behaved the same.
3. The development team confirmed this is how the app currently handles PDFs.
4. Customer reproduced it on several iPhone and Android models. Ruled out: device-specific problem.

## Solution
Expected behavior, no fix. The app shows only a preview of the PDF in the feed. Tapping the Open button hands the file to the phone's PDF viewer, where it opens full screen and can be saved or downloaded.
Full in-app PDF viewing with a download option is not available. Send it as a feature request through the contact form, or ask the Agency team for custom development.
Root cause: the app does not have a full-screen in-app PDF viewer. It shows a preview and passes the file to the device's viewer.

## Customer note
Said the flow still does not meet their need and asked for PDFs to open fully inside the app with a download option.
