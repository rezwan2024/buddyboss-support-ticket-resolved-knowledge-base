## Debugging steps
1. Confirmed the site is on a paid web plan that includes social login, so no upgrade is needed.
2. Ruled out: lost access to the feature. Social login was moved into the separate BuddyBoss Addon plugin, which was not installed on the site.

## Solution
Download the BuddyBoss Addon plugin from the Downloads section of the BuddyBoss account, upload and activate it under Plugins > Add New > Upload Plugin. Then disconnect the BuddyBoss license in the license settings and connect it again with the same key, and reload the social login settings page. Google and Apple sign in become selectable again with the existing configuration.
Root cause: social login moved from the platform into the separate BuddyBoss Addon plugin, so without that plugin the feature shows as a locked upgrade option.
