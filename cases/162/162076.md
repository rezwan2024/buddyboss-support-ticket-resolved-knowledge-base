## Debugging steps
1. Checked the BuddyBoss App settings for an App Next Gen menu or toggle: not present.
2. Checked the installed BuddyBoss App plugin version on the Plugins page: an old version (2.10.0) was running, which has no App Next Gen option.
3. Ruled out: license or account activation. No change on the account side was needed.
4. The update notification had not appeared in the dashboard, likely due to WordPress cron or a temporary connection issue to the update server.

## Solution
Download the latest BuddyBoss App plugin from the BuddyBoss account Downloads page, then go to Plugins > Add New > Upload Plugin, upload the zip and confirm replacing the current version. After the update the toggle appears under BuddyBoss App > App Next Gen.
Root cause: the site ran an old BuddyBoss App plugin version that does not include App Next Gen, and the automatic update notice was not shown.
