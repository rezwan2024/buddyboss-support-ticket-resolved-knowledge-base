## Debugging steps
1. Signing in to YouTube in the app let videos play, but a few hours later the prompt came back.
2. Tested the published app on another Android device. A YouTube post that was already there and a newly added YouTube video both played with no sign-in prompt.
3. Ruled out: account restrictions. The customer said their YouTube account is a normal account with no Restricted Mode and no supervision.
4. Asked the customer to play a public YouTube video from another channel, and to test on a different phone and a different network.

## Solution
No fix confirmed. The prompt could not be reproduced on another device, which points to the device, network or Google account side and not to the app. Next steps were to compare a second Android device and another network, and to collect the phone model and Android version.
Root cause: Not confirmed. YouTube's bot check is likely triggered by the device or network, since the app played the same videos without a prompt on another device.

## Customer note
Said they would test on another Android device to see if it is specific to their phone. No reply after that, and the ticket closed for inactivity.
