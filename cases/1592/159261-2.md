## Debugging steps
1. Installed the most recent Android build from the app dashboard and commented on posts in several groups. The critical error did not appear.
2. Ruled out: reinstalling the app, the error stayed for the customer.
3. Asked for the app version shown in the Play Store on the affected device, to check if the newest build was published to the store.
4. Asked for one example group post and an affected member to test the exact post and account data.

## Solution
Compare the Play Store version on the affected device with the newest build. If the store version is older, publish the newest build and have users update and retest. If it matches, test on the exact failing post and escalate to the app team. The customer did not reply before the ticket auto-closed.
Root cause: not confirmed. Most likely the store still served an older build than the one that tested fine.
