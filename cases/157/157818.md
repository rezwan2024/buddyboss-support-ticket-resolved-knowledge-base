## Debugging steps
1. Checked the request: an outside researcher wants the right contact channel for a security finding and asks about rewards before sharing anything.
2. Ruled out: a site or product malfunction. No details were shared in the ticket and none are needed in support.

## Solution
Send the person to the official security reporting page on the BuddyBoss website and ask them to submit the finding through the form there, not through a support ticket. The security team reviews submissions and replies directly, including about any recognition or rewards.
Root cause: not a product fault; a disclosure request sent to the wrong channel.
