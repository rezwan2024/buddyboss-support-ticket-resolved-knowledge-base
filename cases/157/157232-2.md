## Debugging steps
1. Customer wanted GamiPress points, achievements and rank entries removed from the activity feed, but could not open GamiPress settings because of a critical error.
2. Checked the site and found BuddyBoss Gamification and GamiPress both active at the same time.
3. Ruled out: switching to BuddyBoss Gamification is not required; GamiPress can be kept on its own.

## Solution
Keep only one gamification system. To keep GamiPress, disable the built-in option at BuddyBoss > Settings > BuddyBoss Gamification, then the GamiPress settings load without the error.
Then in GamiPress > Settings > Add-ons, open the BuddyPress/BuddyBoss integration tab and disable the activity option under Points, Achievements and Ranks. Remove old entries in bulk from Dashboard > Activity.
Root cause: BuddyBoss Gamification and GamiPress running together conflict and break the GamiPress settings page.

## Customer note
Said they want to keep GamiPress for its pop-ups and leaderboard shortcodes; no confirmation the error was gone.
