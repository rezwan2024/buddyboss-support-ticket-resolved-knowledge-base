## Debugging steps
1. Confirmed the courses list loads and courses open with their content list, but tapping a lesson or topic shows no content.
2. Asked for the link to one course and the name of a lesson or topic where content does not show in the app.
3. Customer checked again: the content loaded, only very slowly.
4. Ruled out: a known slowdown from the latest BuddyBoss update, since no other reports were found.

## Solution
No change needed. The content loaded after waiting, so it was slow loading and not missing content. If it stays slow, collect which screen was slow and when it happened, and check it on its own.
Root cause: slow response when loading course content in the app, not a broken feature.

## Customer note
Said the courses work again and closed with "All clear."
