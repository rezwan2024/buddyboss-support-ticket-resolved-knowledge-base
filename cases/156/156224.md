## Debugging steps
1. Checked the notice in the admin: it asks to activate the license for the Offload Media and Gamification add-ons, although the license was activated when the add-ons were first released.
2. Checked BuddyBoss > License Activation to see which products show as unlicensed.

## Solution
Go to BuddyBoss > License Activation, scroll to the Troubleshooting section and click Reset License Settings. Then click Activate Your License, enter the license key and the email of the BuddyBoss account, and click Update License. The subscription is not affected by the reset.
Root cause: cached license data on the site went out of sync with the account, so the add-ons showed as not licensed.
