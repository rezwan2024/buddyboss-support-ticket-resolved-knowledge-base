## Debugging steps
1. Asked for the URL of the 404 page to see if the error came from the site or from the BuddyBoss side.
2. Had the customer copy the key from My Account > Licenses and paste it in BuddyBoss > Settings > License; the 404 stopped, but the button only refreshed the dashboard and the license notice stayed.
3. Had the customer install the new BuddyBoss Add-ons plugin and disconnect and reconnect the license key. Ruled out: the notice did not change.
4. Checked the customer's BuddyBoss account and found the wrong license key had been connected on the site.

## Solution
Connect the correct license key from the customer's BuddyBoss account in BuddyBoss > Settings > License. The license notice then cleared.
Root cause: a license key that did not match the active product was connected on the site.
