## Debugging steps
1. Read the error: ApiException 10 is the Google developer error, returned on the phone when the app package name and signing SHA-1 fingerprint do not match an Android OAuth client in the Google Cloud project.
2. Ruled out: the Platform 3.6.1 and Platform Pro 3.3.3 updates. The check fails on the device before the app talks to the website.
3. Ruled out: the Apple sign-in change. Apple and Google sign-in use separate credentials and settings.
4. Asked whether the failing app was the Google Play release or another build. Each is signed differently and needs its own matching client ID.
5. Checked BuddyBoss App > Configure > Android Settings and found the Google Developer account was not connected.

## Solution
In BuddyBoss App > Configure > Android Settings, upload the Google Play service account key (.json) in Connect Your Google Developer Account and click Connect Account until it shows Connected. Follow the docs guide on connecting the Google Developer account to the BuddyBoss App. Then test Google login again and create a new Android build so the app gets the updated configuration.
Root cause: the Google Developer account was not connected in the app's Android settings, so the Google sign-in setup did not match the app's signing.
