## Debugging steps
1. Reproduced with two test members: the blocked member correctly could not see the blocker's posts, comments or profile.
2. Logged in as the blocker: the blocked member's profile could not be opened and the avatar was not clickable, but the blocked member's personal posts still showed in the activity feed.
3. Checked whether the posts were group activity posts, which stay visible by design with the author shown as "Unknown Member". These were personal posts, so that did not explain it.
4. Ruled out: caching. The server cache was cleared and the posts still showed.
5. Confirmed as a known Platform bug and escalated to the development team.

## Solution
Update BuddyBoss Platform to 3.5.1 or later. After that, blocking hides posts in both directions.
Root cause: a Platform bug in the moderation filter on the activity feed hid the blocker's posts from the blocked member, but not the blocked member's posts from the blocker.
