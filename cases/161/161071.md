## Debugging steps
1. Customer reported the Play Console screens no longer match the Android setup guide.
2. Explained the steps and settings are unchanged, only the layout and menu positions moved.
3. Asked which step was blocking: customer could not find the app signing page to get the certificate fingerprint.
4. Documentation team updated the Android App Links guide with the current Play Console screens.

## Solution
In Play Console open the app and go to Protected with Play > Play Store protection > Manage Play app signing, then copy the SHA-256 certificate fingerprint from the App signing key section, not the Upload key certificate section.
Then enable Android App Links in the WordPress dashboard and generate a new Android build, as in the updated "How to configure Android App Links" guide.
Root cause: Google moved the app signing page under Protected with Play in the new Play Console layout.
