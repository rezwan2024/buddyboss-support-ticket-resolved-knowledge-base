## Debugging steps
1. Checked LearnDash LMS > Settings > Notes > New Note Section. Notes were enabled for lessons, and the missing notes were on lessons too.
2. Ruled out: no per-lesson Notes setting exists that could override the global one.
3. Ruled out: plugin conflict. On staging with only BuddyBoss Platform, Platform Pro and the App plugin active, notes still did not show in the app.
4. Ruled out: the instructor-only note viewing option. It controls who can see students' notes, not whether the notes section shows.
5. Sent to the app team. Their review found that the app does not support Notes. Notes do show on the website.

## Solution
No fix in the app. The BuddyBoss App does not support Notes by LearnDash, so the notes section does not show on app course screens for students or instructors.
Workaround: use Notes on the website in a mobile browser while logged in. For app support, the customer was asked to post a feature request on the BuddyBoss roadmap portal (say which note actions and content types they need).
Root cause: the Notes add-on loads notes through admin-ajax, shortcodes and LearnDash web templates, with no REST API, and the app builds course screens only from the REST API.
