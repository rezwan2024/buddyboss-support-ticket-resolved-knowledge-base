## Debugging steps
1. Asked the customer to follow the Apple social login setup guide line by line and report the exact error.
2. Customer had tried the Services ID with and without suffix and edited the private key; first got invalid client errors, then an Apple login prompt when saving in BuddyBoss.
3. Requested Apple Developer admin access to reconfigure; it was an individual (personal) account, so Certificates, Identifiers and Profiles was not visible to the invited user.
4. Asked the customer to start from scratch and follow the setup guide again.

## Solution
Revoke the old Apple key and identifiers in the Apple Developer account, create a new Services ID, key and private key, and enter them again in BuddyBoss Social Login settings following the Apple social login guide. Then test Sign in with Apple.
Root cause: Stale or mismatched Services ID and key configuration between Apple Developer and BuddyBoss.

## Customer note
Said they revoked the old info, restarted with new Service IDs and it is good to go now; Apple login was tested working.
