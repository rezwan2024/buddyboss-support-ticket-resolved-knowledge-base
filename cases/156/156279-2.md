## Debugging steps
1. Checked the plugins list and found two copies each of BuddyBoss Platform and BuddyBoss Platform Pro installed.
2. Confirmed WordPress treats the duplicate copies as competing plugins, so neither copy could be activated.
3. Checked versions: Platform 2.14.4 (current 3.4.2), Platform Pro 2.10.1 (current 3.2.0), Theme 2.14.0 (current 2.21.0), too old for current compatibility testing.

## Solution
Take a full backup, remove the extra copies of Platform and Platform Pro so only one of each remains, then activate them. After that, update Platform, Platform Pro and the Theme to the current versions, ideally on a staging site first because of the large version jump.
Root cause: duplicate installs of the same BuddyBoss plugins blocked activation of both copies.
