## Debugging steps
1. Tested the Legacy and Next Gen builds and played a YouTube post and an uploaded video. Both played, so it could not be reproduced.
2. Asked the customer to log out and back in, reinstall the app, and install the build by scanning the QR code under BuddyBoss App > Builds. The customer still saw the issue.
3. Asked whether it happens on iOS too, whether the same videos play in the phone browser, and whether a VPN or proxy is in use.
4. Retested on a physical Android device instead of a simulator, because the customer said a real device gives a more reliable result.
5. The customer published a new release to Google Play and tested the store version. Uploaded videos played normally.

## Solution
Install the latest published store release, or a fresh build from the current plugin versions, and test on a real device. The customer's uploaded videos played normally in the published release.
Root cause: Not confirmed. Playback worked in the fresh published release, so the older build was the likely cause.

## Customer note
Said in-app uploaded videos play perfectly in the published release. Only the YouTube sign-in prompt was left (see case 160373-2).
