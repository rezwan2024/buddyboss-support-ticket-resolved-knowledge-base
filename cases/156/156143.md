## Debugging steps
1. Customer had already recreated Firebase, Google Cloud, Google Developer and Apple keys, made new builds and checked that the SHA fingerprints and build IDs match. No error is shown in the app.
2. Ruled out: website side. Social login and Create an Account work on the site in a mobile browser; only the apps fail.
3. Asked for admin access to check the app settings and build, but the site did not load from the support location, even over a VPN in another region.
4. Over a VPN in the customer's country the site loaded, which showed the host or a security plugin applies a country-level block before requests reach WordPress.
5. Asked the customer to whitelist the support region on the host or security plugin, since the app connects to the site directly and a VPN does not cover app testing, and to add that region to App Store Connect and Google Play availability or send TestFlight and Android internal testing invites. Also asked for a fresh admin account, as the shared login had expired.

## Solution
No fix was found; the ticket closed for inactivity before the app could be tested. Next step was to lift the country-level block for the tester's region and give app access (store availability or TestFlight and internal testing invites) so the in-app LinkedIn login and registration flows can be reproduced.
Root cause: not confirmed. A country-level block on the site stopped support from reaching it and testing the app.

Last reply asked support to try the site over a VPN in the customer's country; no reply after the request for access and whitelisting.
