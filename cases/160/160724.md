## Debugging steps
1. Read the ticket: an automated alert saying a site visitor asked to speak with a person, with a link to a chat dashboard.
2. Ruled out: no BuddyBoss Platform, Theme or App problem was described.
3. Found the alert came from a third-party chat tool that sends handoff alerts to several emails, including the BuddyBoss support address.

## Solution
Told the customer the visitor can only see replies from their own team in the chat tool's dashboard, so they should reply there.
Advised removing the BuddyBoss support email from the chat tool's notification recipients to stop these alerts.
Root cause: the BuddyBoss support email was listed as a notification recipient in the site's live chat tool.
