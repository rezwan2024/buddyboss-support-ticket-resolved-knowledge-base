## Debugging steps
1. Ruled out: a setting. There is no option under BuddyBoss > Settings > Activity that controls whether group posts show in the main feed.
2. Ruled out: the latest update. The customer said it happened before updating to Platform 3.5.1 and Pro 3.3.2. Caches were also cleared.
3. Tested on a test site with a hidden group. The group post showed in the main Activity feed only for group members and administrators. It did not show for a logged in non-member or a logged out visitor. That is the expected behavior.
4. Checked the customer's screenshots. They confirmed the group was set to Hidden in BuddyBoss > Settings > Social Groups > All Groups (Privacy column).
5. Asked which account took the screenshots (administrator, group member or non-member) and asked them to check the Activity page in a private browser window.

## Solution
Not resolved. The customer did not say which account was seeing the posts. Next step: check the main feed as a logged out visitor and as a non-member. If the post only shows to admins or group members, it is working as designed. If a non-member can see it, check the site directly to find what is different, and repeat the test on the same Platform version.
Root cause: Unknown. Most likely the viewer was an administrator or a group member, who are expected to see hidden group posts in the main feed.

Sent screenshots and said they did not know how to take one while logged out. No reply after that, and the ticket closed for inactivity.
