## Debugging steps
1. Checked Activity Settings under BuddyBoss > Settings and found Relevant Activity enabled.
2. Confirmed that with Relevant Activity on, each member only sees their own posts, connections, followed members, joined groups, subscribed discussions and mentions, so posts and pinned posts reach only some members.
3. Ruled out: group privacy. Turning Relevant Activity off does not change it; posts in private or hidden groups keep the group's privacy and show only to group members.

## Solution
Go to BuddyBoss > Settings > Activity, uncheck Relevant Activity and save. Main feed posts and admin pinned posts then show to all logged-in members, and group posts still show only to members of that group.
Root cause: Relevant Activity limits each member's feed to activity related to them, which hides other members' posts and pinned posts.
