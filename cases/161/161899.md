## Debugging steps
1. Reviewed the outreach email the customer forwarded and the questions about how third parties identified them as a BuddyBoss App customer.
2. Ruled out: BuddyBoss systems as the source. A WordPress site running BuddyBoss Platform, Theme and the BuddyBoss App plugin leaves visible traces in its page source.
3. Checked the usual lead sources: technology lookup services scan sites for these traces and sell lead lists, so a site can be flagged as building a BuddyBoss app before the app reaches the stores.
4. Contact addresses shown on the website, imprint page or business directories are gathered by marketing tools and matched with that technology data.

## Solution
Explained how third parties can find BuddyBoss sites and contact details from public page source, technology lookup services and listed email addresses, without access to BuddyBoss systems. Confirmed that customer data is handled under the BuddyBoss data privacy practices, and passed the concern and the forwarded email to the internal team.
Root cause: Third-party agencies use technology detection tools and website contact details to target BuddyBoss sites. No customer data came from BuddyBoss.
