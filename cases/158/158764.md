## Debugging steps
1. Clicked Send test digest under BuddyBoss > Settings > Emails > Email Digest and got the no pending notifications notice.
2. Checked the admin account's own notification settings: the email digest was Off, so notifications were sent right away and nothing was queued for it.
3. Ruled out: a bug. The test digest is built from the digest notifications waiting for the admin's own account, not from sample content.
4. Customer tagged and followed their own account and still got the notice, because a member's own activity never queues a notification for them.

## Solution
Set the admin account's email digest to Daily or Weekly in its frontend notification settings. From a second account, mention the admin, reply to its post or follow it. Then click Send test digest again; the test goes only to the admin's email and does not clear waiting notifications.
Root cause: Expected behavior; the test digest needs notifications created by other members for the admin account while it is on a Daily or Weekly digest.

Said they still got the same notice after tagging and following their own account; no reply after the second-account steps.
