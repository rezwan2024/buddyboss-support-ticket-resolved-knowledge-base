## Debugging steps
1. Ruled out: app transients, site cache and app cache/data on devices. Clearing them did not remove the message.
2. Checked the subscription in the BuddyBoss account. The renewal was paid late, so the App IDs were disabled, then re-enabled once payment came in.
3. Ruled out: license status. BuddyBoss App > Connect showed the license connected, type Full, and valid.
4. Checked BuddyBoss App > Build. The live Android and iOS builds were many plugin releases old.

## Solution
Update the BuddyBoss App plugin to the latest version. In BuddyBoss App > Build, request new Release builds for Android and iOS, upload them from the Upload tab, and have users update from the stores. If the message stays, disconnect the app key in BuddyBoss App > Connect and connect it again.
Turn on automatic renewal and set a valid default card in the BuddyBoss account billing tab so the App IDs are not disabled again. Each time, the message also cleared on its own a few days after payment, so the steps above were never confirmed as the fix.
Root cause: a missed renewal payment disabled the App IDs, and access did not come back right away after payment, most likely made worse by outdated app builds.
