## Debugging steps
1. Blocked a test member from another member and checked the newsfeed, group feeds and profile.
2. The blocked member could still see and comment on the blocker's posts. Mentions of the blocker showed as @undefined. Only the profile was blocked.
3. Confirmed this as a known Platform bug, and the fix shipped in Platform 3.5.1.
4. After the update, the blocked member could still see and like the blocker's posts in a public group. This is expected when both are members of that group.

## Solution
Update BuddyBoss Platform to 3.5.1 or later, update Platform Pro and the Theme, then clear all caches.
Blocking hides content in the main newsfeed and the profile feed. Group activity still shows when both members belong to the same group. This is by design.
Root cause: A Platform bug where block filtering did not apply to activity feeds and comments. Fixed in 3.5.1.
