## Debugging steps
1. Checked that the pages were selected as public content in the BuddyBoss pages settings and saved the settings again.
2. Cleared all site, server and CDN cache, then tested the page links in a fresh incognito window.
3. Ruled out: browser cache, the redirect to login also happened in private browsing.
4. Asked to deactivate membership, access control and security plugins for a moment, as they can add their own restrictions on top of the BuddyBoss settings.

## Solution
No BuddyBoss change was needed. The issue went away once a separate Stripe payment flow issue on the site was sorted out in another ticket; after that the site no longer asked for card details or login on those pages.
Root cause: the login redirect came from the site's Stripe payment and signup flow, not from the BuddyBoss public pages setting.

## Customer note
Said it was solved already and the core problem was the other Stripe ticket.
