## Debugging steps
1. Confirmed members can change email and password on the website under Settings > Login Information.
2. Confirmed in the app the email field is shown on the account screen but cannot be tapped.
3. Ruled out: plugin conflict. Deactivating the extra plugins and fully reopening the app did not change it.
4. Checked with the app team whether email change is supported in the app.

## Solution
Not a bug, expected behavior. The BuddyBoss App account screen lets members change only their password; the email field is display only. Members change their email on the website under Settings > Login Information. Email change in the app can be requested as a feature request or added to the public roadmap.
Root cause: email change is not a supported feature in the BuddyBoss App, only on the website.

## Customer note
Thanked the team, said the behavior is now clear and asked to pass email change in the app on as a feature request.
