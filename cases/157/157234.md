## Debugging steps
1. Customer could not find the old setting that lets chosen pages be viewed without logging in and said the settings seemed to have changed.
2. Checked where the privacy options now live in the BuddyBoss settings.

## Solution
Go to Dashboard > BuddyBoss > Settings > Advanced > Privacy and manage there which pages are public without login.
Root cause: The privacy setting now sits under the Advanced tab of BuddyBoss Settings, so the customer could not find it in the old place.
