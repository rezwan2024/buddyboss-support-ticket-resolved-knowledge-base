## Debugging steps
1. The customer reported that the live app no longer worked and showed a type error after a recent update.
2. Ruled out: settings changes on the site. Support had only checked the site and changed nothing.
3. Confirmed it was a known issue that many sites saw after the recent update.

## Solution
Download the BuddyBoss addon plugin from the BuddyBoss account, upload and activate it, and enable the needed addons. Update the BuddyBoss App plugin and BuddyBoss Platform, then send a new release build and upload it to the Apple and Google app stores.
Root cause: a known issue in a recent BuddyBoss update. The app needs the BuddyBoss addon plugin active and a new release build.
