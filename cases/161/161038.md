## Debugging steps
1. Logged in as admin and checked the toolbar visibility settings in BuddyBoss Platform.
2. Found "Show the Toolbar for logged-in members (non-admins)" enabled.
3. Ruled out: the custom logo change in Theme Options for the Login/Register page was not related.

## Solution
Go to BuddyBoss > Settings > Advanced > General, Toolbar Settings section, uncheck "Show the Toolbar for logged-in members (non-admins)" and save. The toolbar then shows only for administrators.
Root cause: the Platform option to show the WordPress toolbar to non-admin members was enabled.
