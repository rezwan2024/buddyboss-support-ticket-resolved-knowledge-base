## Debugging steps
1. Checked who was viewing: the customer was logged in as a site admin.
2. Admins and forum moderators can always see hidden forums, so the check must be done logged out in a private window or as a regular member.
3. Checked what was set to hidden: the Hidden visibility setting applies to a forum, not to a single topic.

## Solution
Check the forum logged out or as a regular member. To hide one topic, move it into a forum whose visibility is set to Hidden; a topic inside a public forum stays visible to everyone.
Root cause: Hidden is a forum-level setting that admins and moderators bypass, not a per-topic setting.
