## Debugging steps
1. Confirmed that the WP Fusion settings no longer appear in BuddyBoss Groups after updating BuddyBoss Platform and Theme.
2. Matched it to a known integration issue in the latest BuddyBoss Platform version and escalated to the developers.
3. Developers fixed it and the fix passed QA testing.

## Solution
Update BuddyBoss Platform to version 3.7.0 or later. The WP Fusion settings come back in BuddyBoss Groups.
Root cause: A Platform bug in the previous release stopped the WP Fusion group integration settings from showing in Groups.

## Customer note
Sent a thumbs up to the update that the fix had passed testing. No reply after the release notice.
