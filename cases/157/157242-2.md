## Debugging steps
1. Checked the account: the Web Plus license was enabled, not expired, and assigned to the site.
2. Ruled out: a disabled or expired license on the account side.
3. Checked BuddyBoss, License Keys in wp-admin: the key showed as not connected on the site.
4. After the key was entered, the page showed the license connected and active, and all Platform Pro features were enabled and working.
5. The "features will be disabled soon" notice for BuddyBoss Addons still showed after the license was connected.

## Solution
Enter the license key in BuddyBoss, License Keys in wp-admin and save, then confirm it shows connected and active.
A notice that stays after the key is connected is most likely a cached license status on the site that has not refreshed yet. Nothing gets disabled.
Root cause: the license key was not connected on the site, and the add-ons notice showed a stale cached status.
