## Debugging steps
1. Confirmed activities live in BuddyBoss's own database table, not as WordPress posts, so SEO plugin sitemaps such as Rank Math skip them.
2. Confirmed BuddyBoss has no built-in sitemap for activity posts. BuddyPress Power SEO covers members and groups, not activities.
3. Checked Site SEO (Platform Pro): BuddyBoss > Settings > Appearance > Site SEO > Indexing has toggles for Posts, Profiles and Groups.
4. Ruled out Private Network: when it is on, logged-out visitors and Googlebot are sent to the login page.

## Solution
Turn on the Posts toggle in Site SEO > Indexing and keep Private Network off. For discovery, build a custom sitemap in a separate plugin, no core edits. Use bp_activity_get() with privacy set to public (it skips spam and hide_sitewide items by default) and leave out private or hidden group activity. bp_activity_user_can_read() checks group access and spam but not the activity's own privacy value. Build URLs with bp_activity_get_permalink() (/activity/p/ID/) and list the final member or group URL it redirects to. You can also send an activity sitemap feature request.
Root cause: activities are not WordPress posts, so standard sitemaps do not list them and BuddyBoss has no activity sitemap yet.
