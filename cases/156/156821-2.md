## Debugging steps
1. Customer downloaded a course and switched to Flight Mode; the course opened at first.
2. After some time, or after refreshing or reloading the app, all courses became inaccessible, including content already on the device.
3. Reported in the same ticket as the offline audio bug, while that bug was being fixed.

## Solution
No separate fix was given for this issue. The final reply asked to update the BuddyBoss App plugin and BuddyBoss Platform plugin, request a new app build and upload it, then recheck; it is not confirmed that this covered the offline access loss.
Root cause: not confirmed in the ticket.

## Customer note
No reply after the update and rebuild instructions were sent.
