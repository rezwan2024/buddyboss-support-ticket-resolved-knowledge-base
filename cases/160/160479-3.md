## Debugging steps
1. Checked whether non-BuddyBoss WordPress pages can appear in the app.
2. Confirmed any WordPress page, including Elementor pages, can be added to the app as a menu item and is displayed inside the app.
3. Noted that pages limited to admins and the individual user rely on the user being logged in, which is the case in the app.

## Solution
Add the WordPress or Elementor page to the app menu in BuddyBoss App settings. Test pages with custom functionality inside the app before launch to confirm they work as expected.
Root cause: Not a fault; the app only shows pages that are added to its menus.
