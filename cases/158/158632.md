## Debugging steps
1. Checked the site front end as a visitor: it loaded normally, including the groups and resources sections.
2. Ruled out: a site-wide outage. The problem was limited to logged-in actions (adding members to groups, uploading documents and videos).
3. Asked for a screen recording of both failures, the versions before and after the update, and temporary admin access.
4. The site admin found that the permalinks needed to be updated after the update.

## Solution
In WordPress admin go to Settings > Permalinks and save the permalinks again (update them) so the rewrite rules are rebuilt. Adding members to groups and uploading documents and videos then worked again.
Root cause: rewrite rules were out of date after the update, so the logged-in group member and media upload actions failed.

## Customer note
Thanked the team and said their admin fixed it by updating the permalinks.
