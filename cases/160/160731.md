## Debugging steps
1. Looked up the account by the purchase email, since the contact email had no account or orders.
2. Checked the purchase history: BuddyBoss Web Lifetime Deal bought through the old three-payment installment plan.
3. Found the third and final payment went through, but the order was never switched to lifetime status, so the license showed as cancelled or expired.

## Solution
Manually update the license to lifetime status in the account. The customer can then download the latest versions from BuddyBoss.com, My Account, Downloads.
Root cause: the old installment plan did not automatically convert the order to a lifetime license after the final payment.

## Customer note
Said they could see it was fixed.
