## Debugging steps
1. Checked the customer's screenshot: it showed the Edit Email Template window, not the digest settings.
2. Ruled out: a checkbox bug. The Situation checkboxes in the email template only pick which event sends that single email, so they are not digest options.
3. Pointed to the real digest settings page and its requirements.

## Solution
Cancel the email template edit. Go to BuddyBoss > Settings > Notifications > Settings > Email Digest tab and turn on the categories to include: Social, Group Updates, Private Messages (members must also opt in to private messages). The same tab sets daily or weekly delivery, send time, an optional heading, and has Send Test Digest.
Existing members keep getting individual emails until they pick Daily or Weekly under Account Settings > Notification Settings. Needs Platform 3.5.0, BuddyBoss Addons 1.2.0 and the Start plan or above; no Email Digest tab means one of these is missing.
Root cause: the customer was editing an email template's Situation checkboxes instead of the Email Digest settings tab.
