## Debugging steps
1. Reviewed the Apple App Review rejection message about Sign in with Apple on the login screen.
2. Checked the app login screen; the background image made the Sign in with Apple and other SSO buttons appear transparent.
3. Confirmed this is a bug in the BuddyBoss App and reported it to the development team.

## Solution
Workaround: go to BuddyBoss App > Appearance > Branding, under Login Screen remove the Login Background image and save. No rebuild is needed; check the login screen on a phone, then ask Apple to review the app again. The background image can be added back once the bug is fixed.
Root cause: A bug where the login screen background image renders the SSO buttons, including Sign in with Apple, as transparent.

## Customer note
Said the buttons were visible after removing the background image, resubmitted to Apple and considered it resolved.
