## Debugging steps
1. Checked that Apple Social Login is enabled in BuddyBoss Platform > Settings > General > Registration.
2. Checked the date of the latest iOS build and whether Apple Social Login was enabled before that build was made.
3. Checked that Sign in with Apple is enabled on the App ID in the Apple Developer portal.
4. Installed the latest build on a device: the Apple login button was there, so the build Apple had reviewed was an older one made before Social Login was set up.

## Solution
Request a new iOS build after enabling Apple Social Login, then upload that build and submit it to Apple for review. Social Login changes only reach the app in a new build, and when Social Login is on, both Google and Apple login are needed to publish to the app stores.
Root cause: The build that was submitted to Apple was made before Apple Social Login was enabled, so it did not include the Sign in with Apple button.

## Customer note
Said the Apple button now shows after the new build, the app was approved by Apple, and thanked the team.
