## Debugging steps
1. Member set Weekly digest but still received an individual email right away.
2. Checked the Email Digest tab: Email Digest itself must be enabled and the Social and Group Updates categories switched on; only notifications in enabled categories are held.
3. Ruled out: a bug for connection requests, invitations and account emails, which always send right away.
4. Checked default frequency: it only applies to members who register after the digest is enabled.

## Solution
Enable Email Digest and the wanted categories in the Email Digest tab. Existing members stay on individual emails until they pick Daily or Weekly under Account Settings > Notification Settings; there is no setting to switch all existing members at once, so announce the option to members.
Root cause: Digest only holds enabled categories, some emails always send at once, and the default frequency only applies to new registrations.
