## Debugging steps
1. Reviewed the customer screenshots: JS exception raised from the activity widget on iOS.
2. Asked for an affected user login and WP-Admin access; only admin access was given.
3. Ruled out: a site configuration problem, the iOS app opened and worked fine when tested by support.

## Solution
Generate a new iOS build, publish it, and have the affected users update and test again. Not confirmed by the customer.
Root cause: Not confirmed; most likely users were on an older build that did not match the current site and App plugin.

## Customer note
Said reports came in until the weekend before, then could not reproduce the error on iOS themselves.
