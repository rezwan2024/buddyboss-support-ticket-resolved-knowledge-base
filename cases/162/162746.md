## Debugging steps
1. Read the message: a WordPress developer offering content upload, branding and update services in reply to a job post.
2. Ruled out: no BuddyBoss product question, error or account request in the message.

## Solution
Reply politely that this inbox is the BuddyBoss technical support channel for product questions and job applications cannot be reviewed here. Ask the sender to apply through the website or platform where they saw the job post, then close the ticket.
Root cause: a job application was sent to the support inbox by mistake.

## Customer note
No reply after the redirect.
