## Debugging steps
1. Customer reported many broken layouts on the Tutor LMS pages after updating Tutor LMS from 3.9 to 4.x on the BuddyBoss Theme and Platform.
2. Matched it to other reports of the same Tutor LMS 4.x layout and dashboard compatibility problem with the BuddyBoss Theme.
3. Confirmed it as a known bug in the theme's Tutor LMS integration and sent it to the development team for a fix.
4. Workaround until the fix came out: stay on Tutor LMS 3.9.

## Solution
Update the BuddyBoss Theme to v2.22.4 or later, which contains the Tutor LMS 4.x compatibility fix.
Until the theme can be updated, stay on Tutor LMS 3.9 (workaround).
Root cause: the BuddyBoss Theme's Tutor LMS styles and templates were built for Tutor LMS 3.x and did not match the changed Tutor LMS 4.x layout.
