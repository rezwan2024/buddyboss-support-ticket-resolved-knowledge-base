## Debugging steps
1. Checked BuddyBoss Courses course settings, the lesson editor and the email settings for drip or scheduled unlock options.
2. Ruled out: no unlock by days after enrolment, no unlock on set calendar dates, no lock of future lessons until release and no automatic unlock emails in BuddyBoss Courses yet.

## Solution
BuddyBoss Courses does not support drip scheduling yet. Keep LearnDash with a cohort or drip plugin, which stays fully supported through the BuddyBoss LearnDash integration, for weekly unlocks, locked future weeks and unlock emails.
Suggest the customer submit drip scheduling as a feature request through the BuddyBoss contact form.
Root cause: BuddyBoss Courses is a new feature and drip scheduling is not built in yet.
