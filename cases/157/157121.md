## Debugging steps
1. Ruled out: browser cache and cookies, the survey loop also happened in incognito and in another browser.
2. The survey state was reset on the member's community account so the survey only shows once every 90 days.
3. After the reset the member was still redirected to the survey page after login, which now showed a 404.
4. Ruled out: the member account itself, a fresh test account on the community site logged in fine.

## Solution
Reset (flush) the permalinks on the community site: Settings > Permalinks > Save Changes. This fixed the redirect to the 404 survey page.
Root cause: rewrite rules were not flushed after a change, possibly the Platform plugin or another plugin not flushing permalinks when an action needed it.

Said they were still getting the same 404 after login; no reply after the permalink reset and the ticket closed for inactivity.
