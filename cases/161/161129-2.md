## Debugging steps
1. Checked which faster signup options are built in.
2. Ruled out: SMS one-time code verification, not a built-in feature.

## Solution
Enable BuddyBoss Social Login at BuddyBoss > Settings > General > Registration, then add the Client ID and Client Secret from a Google Cloud app. Apple, Facebook, X and LinkedIn are also supported. For SMS codes a third-party plugin or custom development is needed.
Root cause: Google signup is built in through Social Login but was not enabled; SMS verification is not offered.
