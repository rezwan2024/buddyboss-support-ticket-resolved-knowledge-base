## Debugging steps
1. Confirmed the BuddyBoss login redirect is set to the News Feed, yet regular members who open /wp-admin/ land on WooCommerce My Account.
2. Admins still need dashboard access and WooCommerce has to stay active, so turning WooCommerce off is not an option.
3. Checked the earlier ticket: the inactivity workflow closed it automatically. No team member marked it resolved.

## Solution
Not solved in this ticket. Support passed the open questions (test results on a standard setup, expected behavior, recommended setup) to the agent on the original ticket, which was reopened to carry on the investigation.
Possible lead, not confirmed: WooCommerce sends non-admins away from /wp-admin/ to My Account by itself. The BuddyBoss login redirect only acts after the login form is sent. A custom redirect for non-admins on /wp-admin/ could point them to the News Feed instead.
Root cause: not confirmed. Most likely WooCommerce's own block on dashboard access for non-admins, which works apart from the BuddyBoss login redirect.
