## Debugging steps
1. Customer has courses built as one Elementor page template with all content on a single page.
2. Checked whether a BuddyBoss course needs lessons and topics to work.

## Solution
A BuddyBoss course does not require lessons. Create the course and keep all content on the course page itself.
Without lessons or topics there is no progress tracking, completion status or anything that depends on curriculum item completion.
Root cause: progress and completion in BuddyBoss Courses are driven by lessons and topics, not by the course page.
