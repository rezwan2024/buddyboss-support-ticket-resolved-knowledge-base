## Debugging steps
1. Compared the homepage with Forums, News Feed and Members pages: homepage used the BuddyBoss Theme templates, community pages used ReadyLaunch templates.
2. Checked Dashboard > BuddyBoss > Appearance > Settings > General > Site Layout: ReadyLaunch was enabled while the BuddyBoss Theme was also active.
3. Ruled out: a broken theme setting. Theme Options do not apply to ReadyLaunch templates by design, since ReadyLaunch works independently of the theme.

## Solution
Go to Dashboard > BuddyBoss > Appearance > Settings > General > Site Layout and switch Site Layout away from ReadyLaunch. BuddyBoss Theme Options (menus, header, colors) then apply to Forums, News Feed and other community pages too. Clear caches after the change.
Root cause: ReadyLaunch was enabled on a site already running the BuddyBoss Theme, so community pages used ReadyLaunch templates that ignore Theme Options.
