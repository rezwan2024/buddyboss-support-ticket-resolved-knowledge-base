## Debugging steps
1. Confirmed the forwarded email is a general notice Google sends to every Play developer, not a BuddyBoss task.
2. Google already registered most existing Play apps automatically with their Play signing keys, so the email is usually just a confirmation prompt.
3. Ruled out: iOS app, as this requirement applies to Android only.

## Solution
The app owner checks it in their own Google Play Console: on the Home page, check the package name status next to the app (a filter shows unregistered apps), then under Settings open the Android developer verification page and confirm the developer identity shows as verified.
If the app shows as unregistered or a draft registration exists, complete it from the same page. Send a screenshot to support if help is needed with the package name or signing key fields.
Root cause: Google's new Android developer verification requirement, handled by the Play Console account owner and not by BuddyBoss.
