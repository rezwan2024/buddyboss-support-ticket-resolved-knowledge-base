## Debugging steps
1. Checked Settings > About in the live app for the site URL and version, since the site URL is fixed at build time while most app settings load from the site at runtime.
2. Fully closed and reopened the app and logged out and back in to clear the locally cached configuration.
3. Compared the live App Store app with a TestFlight test build.
4. In App Store Connect, checked that Agreements, Tax, and Banking is Active (if not, production gets no purchase products at all), that the product ID matches the BuddyBoss App in-app purchase settings exactly, and whether the product was submitted with an app version or approved later.

## Solution
After these checks the Pro product showed on the Products page and during registration in both the live app and TestFlight, with no new release build. The ticket does not say which single check fixed it, so run all of them before rebuilding.
Root cause: not confirmed; likely cached app configuration or App Store Connect product availability catching up after approval.

## Customer note
Thanked the team and said the Pro product visibility issue appears to be resolved in both production and TestFlight.
