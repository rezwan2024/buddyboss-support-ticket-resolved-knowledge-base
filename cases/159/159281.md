## Debugging steps
1. Ruled out: the Zoom Gutenberg block. It is a separate way to show a meeting on a page and is not needed for group meetings, so its timezone crash in the editor was not the cause.
2. Ruled out: the Server-to-Server OAuth app in the Zoom App Marketplace. It was activated, had credentials, Secret Token, all required scopes and a validated event subscription URL.
3. Checked BuddyBoss > Settings > Integrations > Zoom on the site: the Zoom In-Browser Meetings section was connected with the same Client ID and Client Secret as the Server-to-Server OAuth app.
4. Created a short test meeting in the group: the countdown was correct and "Join Meeting in Browser" appeared 10 minutes before start, but clicking it gave the signature invalid error. With In-Browser Meetings enabled, that button is the only way to join.

## Solution
In the Zoom App Marketplace, go to Develop > Build App and create a new General App. Under Features > Embed, turn on Meeting SDK, then copy that app's Client ID and Client Secret from App Credentials.
In BuddyBoss > Settings > Integrations > Zoom > Zoom In-Browser Meetings, disconnect, paste the Meeting SDK app keys, click Connect and keep "Enabled for" set to Meetings. Leave the Server-to-Server OAuth app connected in the Zoom Gutenberg Blocks section. If joining through the Zoom app is fine, a quicker option is to set "Enabled for" to None.
Root cause: the In-Browser Meetings section needs keys from a separate Meeting SDK app, and the Server-to-Server OAuth keys make Zoom reject the in-browser join signature.
