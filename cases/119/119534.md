## Debugging steps
1. Ruled out: app transients, site cache and app cache on devices. Clearing them did not remove the message.
2. Checked the order in the BuddyBoss account. A missed renewal payment had disabled the App IDs. They were enabled again once the payment came through.
3. Ruled out: license status. The Connect tab showed the license as connected, type Full and valid.
4. On the Build tab, found that the live Android and iOS builds were many releases older than the BuddyBoss App plugin.

## Solution
1. Pay the renewal, then turn on auto-renew and set the right default card in the account Billing tab so the App IDs are not disabled again.
2. If the message stays, go to BuddyBoss App > Connect, disconnect the app key and connect it again.
3. Update the BuddyBoss App plugin to the latest version. Request new Release builds for Android and iOS, upload them to the stores and have users update the app.
Root cause: A missed renewal disabled the App IDs. After payment, the app still showed the message for some days. An old app build may be a factor, but this was not confirmed.
