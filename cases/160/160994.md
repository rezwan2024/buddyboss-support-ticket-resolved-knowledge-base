## Debugging steps
1. Confirmed the theme customizations show on normal pages but not on community pages (Account, Forums, Groups, Members).
2. Checked which layout the community pages use: BuddyBoss Theme or ReadyLaunch.
3. ReadyLaunch was styling the community pages, so BuddyBoss Theme settings did not reach them.

## Solution
Turn off ReadyLaunch for the community pages so the BuddyBoss Theme handles them, then clear caches. If ReadyLaunch should stay on, style those pages with the ReadyLaunch settings in BuddyBoss Platform instead.
Root cause: ReadyLaunch styles community pages on its own and overrides BuddyBoss Theme customizations there.
