## Debugging steps
1. Asked which user role the affected members have: Subscriber.
2. Confirmed from the customer's screenshot that Subscribers see no three-dot menu at all on their own discussions.
3. Found that the three-dot menu for members only holds Edit, so it is hidden when discussion editing is turned off.

## Solution
In the WordPress dashboard go to BuddyBoss > Settings > Forums and enable discussion editing for members. Subscribers then see the three-dot menu with Edit on their own discussions.
Root cause: Discussion editing for members was turned off in the forum settings, so the options menu had no items and was hidden.

## Customer note
Thanked for the clarification but said enabling editing alone does not meet their need to let members delete their own discussions.
