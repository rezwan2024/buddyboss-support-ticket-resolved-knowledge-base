## Debugging steps
1. Reviewed the customer's Google Play Console screenshots: policy status shows no issues, website, contact email and phone are verified.
2. Confirmed the app package name is listed under Package names in the Android developer verification section.
3. Ruled out: any pending step on the BuddyBoss side for the next app update.

## Solution
Tell the customer to finish both parts of Google's Android developer verification: complete the Identity tab next to Package names, and confirm the app signing key is registered under the package name (with Play App Signing it is normally listed automatically).
Explain that BuddyBoss does not manage the Google Play Console account. The Publishing Service covers build upload and submission, not account verification.
Root cause: Google requires both identity verification and package name plus signing key registration by the deadline, and the notice shows until both are complete.

Thanked the team for the update and said they are still checking with their team.
