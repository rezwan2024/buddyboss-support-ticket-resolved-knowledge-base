## Debugging steps
1. Checked the request against core features: BuddyBoss has no per-member store, points or balance system, or product requests between friends.
2. Ruled out: turning the Documents tab into a store through settings. It handles files, not products, prices or balances.
3. Confirmed the parts already native: profile photo albums, document folders, and activity posts with reactions and comments.

## Solution
Enable profile albums and folders in BuddyBoss > Settings > Media Uploading (Photos: "Enable albums in profiles"; Documents section for folders).
For the store, use WooCommerce with a multi-vendor plugin (Dokan or WCFM) plus a bridge plugin such as BuddyVendor to show each member's products on their profile and post new products to the activity feed. These are untested third-party plugins, so try them on staging first. Points, balance and friend product requests need custom development (BuddyBoss Agency) or a feature request.
Root cause: Per-member stores are not a BuddyBoss core feature.
